Twinsburg Sports Calendar Reports

About This Project

This community project turns Twinsburg High School sports schedules into a convenient digital calendar and downloadable PDF reports. It brings events from the available team schedules into one place, making it easier for students, families, staff, and community members to follow upcoming games and activities. Read more about how and why it was created in Turning Paper Sports Schedules into a Useful Digital Calendar and PDF Report.

Questions about this project may be emailed to tj@tjpowell.net.

If you are using this project or find it helpful, I would appreciate hearing from you. Please email me at tj@tjpowell.net.

Subscribe to the Calendar

To receive future schedule updates automatically, subscribe to the iCalendar feed instead of opening or downloading it. Copy the complete address shown below. It is intentionally displayed as plain text because clicking an .ics address normally downloads a current copy rather than creating a calendar subscription.

https://tjpowellnet.github.io/Twinsburg-High-School-Sports-Calendar/THS-Sports.ics

In Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or another calendar application, choose an option such as Add Subscription Calendar, New Calendar Subscription, or Subscribe from URL. Paste the copied address when prompted. A true subscription allows your calendar application to receive later changes to dates, times, and locations.
